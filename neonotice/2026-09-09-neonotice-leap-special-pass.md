**How to use**
1. Click the Soul Pass (F11) icon.
2. Click the Leap Special Pass tab.
3. Complete missions to earn points.
4. Every 1,000 points raises your pass level by 1; reaching a pass level
   grants that level's reward (up to level 10). *Use the Claim All button to
   claim every earned level's rewards at once.*
5. Use the ticket to purchase Soul Pass Plus for premium rewards.

*The Leap Special Pass Ticket can be used on the Jin/Lyn servers; the
Specialization Leap Special Pass Ticket can be used on the Gold server.*
*Earning points via pass level-up purchases, and purchasing pass level-ups
directly, are both unavailable on the Leap Special Pass / Specialization Leap
Special Pass.*
*Activating Soul Pass Plus benefits on the Leap Special Pass / Specialization
Leap Special Pass does not grant the regular Soul Pass's convenience
features.*
