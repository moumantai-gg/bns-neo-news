# September 9, 2026 'Boost to NEO! +1!' Event Guide

Translated from the official [event notice](https://bns.plaync.com/board/neonotice/view?articleId=6a9fe571d97eae18cc40ddf6),
posted 2026-09-09 02:20 KST.

Greetings, Warriors.

For new/returning Warriors beginning a new journey, and existing Warriors who
will be a reliable guide for them, the "Boost to NEO! +1!" event begins,
packed with special referral benefits.

Grow quickly on the Boost Server, then transfer to the Jin/Lyn Server for a
bountiful settlement gift!
Check the details below.

- **Event period:** After the September 9 (Wed), 2026 regular maintenance –
  before the September 30 (Wed), 2026 regular maintenance (3 weeks)
- **Eligibility:**

| Category | Eligibility criteria | Target server |
|---|---|---|
| New account | Created after 06:00 on August 19 (Wed), 2026 | Boost Server |
| Returning account | No login activity between 06:00 on July 20 (Mon), 2026 and 06:00 on August 19 (Wed), 2026 | Boost Server |
| Existing account | Any account not meeting the New/Returning criteria above | Jin / Lyn Server |

*New/returning accounts already on the Boost Server before the event starts
are also eligible, provided they meet the criteria.*

- **How to participate:**
  1. Create a character on the Boost Server with a new/returning account.
  2. Submit the referring existing Warrior's information via the official
     website's [Customer Support > 1:1 Inquiry], using the form below.
     [Event participation shortcut]

**1:1 Inquiry form**
- Inquiry title: `[Boost to NEO! +1!] Event Participation Request`
- Inquiry details:
  1. Referring character's server: choose one of Jin / Lyn
  2. Referring character's name: (enter the referrer's character name exactly)

*Only one 1:1 inquiry submission is accepted per new/returning account, and
the referrer cannot be changed after submission. Referring characters must be
on the Jin/Lyn Server.*

- **Reward guide:**

**EVENT 1. A reward for the Warrior who forged a new bond! (for existing accounts)**
- Rewards are granted based on how many new/returning accounts that
  registered you as their referrer graduate from the Boost Server and
  complete transfer to the Jin/Lyn Server.
- Up to 2 characters per account can transfer from the Boost Server, but the
  event count is tallied per account.
- Referring an existing account with a new/returning account under the same
  identity is not recognized as valid participation.
- If 4 referred accounts complete the transfer, the milestone rewards for
  accounts 1 through 4 are all granted.
- This reward is granted once per account, sent sequentially as an in-account
  push reward after regular maintenance.

| Accounts transferred | Reward | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Radiant Seal Box | 1 |
| 2 | Radiant Special Seal Box | 1 |
| 3 | Black Dragon Bound Skill Book Box (Legendary) | 1 |
| 4 | Premium Dawncrest Portrait Box (Legendary) | 1 |

**EVENT 2. A transfer celebration and Supreme Force milestone gift! (for new/returning accounts)**
- New/returning Warriors who registered a referrer via 1:1 inquiry and settle
  into the Jin/Lyn Server after passing through the Boost Server receive a
  special growth gift.
- This reward is granted once per account, sent sequentially as an in-account
  push reward after regular maintenance.

| Mission | Mission condition | Reward | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer complete | Complete character transfer from the Boost Server to the Jin/Lyn Server | Black Dragon Bound Skill Book Box (Legendary) | 1 |
| Target Supreme Force reached | Reach highest Supreme Force 32,500 after transfer | Premium Dawncrest Portrait Box (Legendary) | 1 |
| | | Sagewood Soul Shield Box | 1 |

- **Reward delivery:** sent sequentially, in the afternoon, after each weekly
  regular maintenance.

| Week | Mission qualifying period | Reward delivery date |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | After the September 9 (Wed), 2026 maintenance – before the September 16 (Wed), 2026 maintenance | After the September 16 (Wed), 2026 maintenance |
| Week 2 | After the September 16 (Wed), 2026 maintenance – before the September 23 (Wed), 2026 maintenance | After the September 23 (Wed), 2026 maintenance |
| Week 3 | After the September 23 (Wed), 2026 maintenance – before the September 30 (Wed), 2026 maintenance | After the September 30 (Wed), 2026 maintenance |

**※ Cautions**
1. This event is tracked and counted per account; participation via multiple
   accounts under the same identity, or through abnormal means, may result in
   exclusion from rewards or reward recovery.
2. The event schedule or rewards may change during the event due to internal
   circumstances.
3. If the referrer information (server/character name) submitted by a
   new/returning Warrior is incorrect, or names a character that doesn't
   exist, it's excluded from the referral count.
4. The Supreme Force milestone mission is checked against the point before
   each weekly regular maintenance, after server transfer is complete.
5. Event reward items are distributed sequentially after regular maintenance,
   and can be received until 05:00 on October 7 (Wed), 2026.
6. Sanctioned accounts cannot participate in this event.
7. This event is only available on the NEO and Boost servers.
8. This event follows the [Blade & Soul Event Terms].

Thank you.
