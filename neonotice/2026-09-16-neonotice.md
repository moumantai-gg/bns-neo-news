**How to participate**
1. Click Soul Pass (F11).
2. Click the [Purchase Soul Pass Plus] button to unlock Soul Pass Premium
   rewards.
   - Soul Pass Premium benefits apply per character (once per character).
   - However, within Soul Pass Plus's convenience features listed below,
     the benefit is shared with same-server characters on your account (a
     NEO-server character's purchase is shared with your other NEO-server
     characters; a Specialization-server character's purchase is shared
     with your other Specialization-server characters): Remote Storage use,
     Soul Storage use, and instant use of stored Warehouse items as
     refining/transmute/crafting materials.
   - *Soul Pass Plus's convenience features apply from purchase until
     before the October 21, 2026 regular maintenance.*
   - NEO server: 2,400 Divine Gems. Specialization server: Specialization
     Soul Pass Ticket — purchasable at the BnS Shop (F10), usable only once
     per character, usable until before the October 21, 2026 regular
     maintenance.
3. Complete missions to reach pass levels and earn their rewards.
4. For faster pass leveling, click the [Pass Level Up] button to earn Soul
   Pass Points.
   - Pass level-up purchases are only available up to Soul Pass level 30.
   - Each pass level-up purchase grants 1,000 Soul Pass Points.
   - NEO server: 110 Divine Gems. Specialization server: Specialization
     Soul Pass Level-Up — purchasable up to 30 times per character via the
     Dragon Express button at the bottom of Inventory (I), usable until
     before the October 21, 2026 regular maintenance.
