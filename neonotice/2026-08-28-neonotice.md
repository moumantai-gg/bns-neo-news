## Director's Letter — Post-Update Changes & Improvement Direction

We thank all our customers who love Blade & Soul NEO. Here's an update on the
changes and improvement direction following recent updates.

### 1. August 26 Update Changes

**Mushin's Hall > Mushin's Tower**
- Floors 5–6: Hongmoon Blessing consumption changed to 0; only normal material
  rewards obtainable there now.
- Floor 7: killing NPC "Junghado" now consumes 12 Hongmoon Blessing, with a changed
  kill reward.

**Mushin's Hall > Silverfrost Trials**
- Floors 1–2: Hongmoon Blessing consumption changed to 0; only normal material
  rewards obtainable there now.
- Floor 3: killing NPC "Raging Yeti Manifestation" now consumes 12 Hongmoon
  Blessing, with a changed kill reward.

**Quests > Daily Quest**
- Removed the Mushin's Tower-related daily quest.
- Added the "Final Training" daily quest.

**◆ Mushin's Tower play & Ductile Stone supply improvements**

Ductile Stone is an essential material for gear growth, and we've seen many
Daehyeop steadily using Mushin's Hall — including Mushin's Tower and Silverfrost
Trials — every day. We also recognized that the existing structure was adding
play fatigue: Hongmoon Blessing was consumed piecemeal across every floor, and
rewards including Ductile Stone were scattered, so getting what you actually
wanted meant checking every floor's rewards and repeatedly clearing all of them —
with many Daehyeop feeling burdened by Blessing being consumed even on floors
they didn't need. So we reduced Blessing consumption on floors that felt
unnecessary, and restructured things so the rewards that actually matter are more
reliably obtainable:

1. **Consolidated Hongmoon Blessing consumption, concentrated on key rewards** —
   Mushin's Tower floors 5–6 and Silverfrost Trials floors 1–2 no longer consume
   Blessing. Blessing is now consumed on defeating the final bosses, Junghado and
   Raging Yeti Manifestation, easing the burden of reaching the final floor.
2. **Improved Ductile Stone supply** — Raised the Ductile Stone drop chance from
   defeating Naksun (Trial Chamber) and Yunsang (Training Chamber), for steadier,
   more reliable supply as you grow.
3. **Added "Final Training" to the daily quest** — This season changed the
   Mushin's Tower daily quest to target a different floor based on character
   Supreme Force, but in practice growing further just raised the required floor,
   increasing daily-quest burden. Since Mushin's Tower is solo content where
   difficulty depends heavily on player skill and gear, we reverted to the
   earlier "Final Training" format: regardless of Supreme Force, you can pick
   whichever of Junghado, Naksun, Yunsang, or Hongseokgeun suits you to complete
   the quest. We've also added a remote-completion convenience feature to ease
   the burden of daily repeat content.

We'll keep watching for real play friction and keep improving accordingly.

**System > Path of Growth**
- Once Path of Growth is fully complete, the Boost Mission window now opens
  automatically. *(If there are no Boost Missions, the Guidebook window opens
  instead.)*

**◆ Strengthened guide flow after completing Path of Growth**

We improved the guide flow so Daehyeop starting fresh or returning via the Boost
Server don't struggle to find their next goal after finishing Path of Growth —
the Boost Mission window now opens immediately on completion, showing the next
growth goal without a separate search.

### 2. August 28 Update Changes

**[System improvement] Server Transfer**
- On Boost Server transfer, the target server is no longer auto-selected.

**[Interface improvement] Character Creation & Server Select**
- New/returning users now see the server-select screen directly on login.
- Added Boost Server guidance to the Neo/Specialization server character-creation
  flow.

**◆ Preventing server-transfer mis-selection; strengthening new/returning-Daehyeop
entry flow**

We've seen some Daehyeop mis-select a server during Boost Server transfer, or get
confused finding the Boost Server. We've reinforced the related support and
guidance so growth on the Boost Server and settling in after transfer go more
smoothly. See [2026-08-28-neonotice.md](2026-08-28-neonotice.md) for details.

1. **Wrong-transfer-target support** — The transfer target server is no longer
   auto-selected. If you already transferred to the wrong server, you can recover
   one Boost Server transfer attempt by having the mis-transferred character
   deleted (via 1:1 inquiry).
2. **[Boost Plus Settlement Support Box] added after transfer** — Starting after
   the September 2 regular maintenance, a "Boost Plus Settlement Support Box"
   will be granted so play continues smoothly even after finishing growth on the
   Boost Server. Daehyeop who completed their server transfer on August 26 will
   also receive it by mail after the September 2 maintenance.
3. **Stronger server guidance for new/returning Daehyeop** — The server-select
   window now appears immediately for new/returning accounts on login; Boost
   Server guidance was also reinforced in character creation and character
   select.

### Future Update Plans & Improvement Direction

**1. Full overhaul of the Challenge Reward system**

◆ Reworking the Supreme Force requirement and reward-count-limit method for
Challenge Rewards

We recognize the issue many Daehyeop have raised about Challenge Box acquisition
conditions, and want to explain our planned rework direction. Currently, a party
only counts as a "Challenge party" if the party's *average* Supreme Force falls
between the dungeon's recommended and minimum Supreme Force. Though designed to
encourage cooperation and achievement, in practice a high-Supreme-Force Daehyeop
joining a party raises the average past the Challenge threshold — leading players
to pause their own growth just to keep earning Challenge rewards, or to hesitate
about partying with others at all, undercutting the fun of both growth and
cooperation. We're retiring the "party average Supreme Force" standard in favor
of: the whole party counts as a Challenge party as long as *at least one* member
meets the dungeon's Supreme Force requirement. Since this does widen reward
eligibility, total reward supply will also rise — so alongside expanding earning
opportunities, we're considering a maximum-reward-count cap to keep the economy
balanced. Exact counts and rollout timing will be shared once we've simulated the
effect on reward supply and the economy.

**2. Clearer growth guidance**

◆ Improved gear-option reroll (stat change) feedback — clearer before/after
combat-power delta display, with visual/color emphasis, when rerolling stats like
Crit or Accuracy.

◆ Improved skill-book info display and selection guidance — tooltips will show
every usable skill instead of just one, and there'll be an at-a-glance guide to
the skill tree available on first equip.

**3. Dungeon & field play polish**

◆ Field-boss spawn guidance and hunting-environment improvements — working on a
way to show field-boss spawn regions clearly without cluttering the HUD, while
also curbing excessive repeat-hunting via channel-hopping (without disrupting
normal farming routines like weekly-quest completion).

◆ Reduced dungeon-gimmick fatigue, more distinct named NPCs — raised gadget
damage in Riotous Passion Sky Park for a faster path to its Special Sequence,
shortening overall clear time; will sharpen the combat identity of key NPCs like
Darkstorm Training Ground's mid-boss.

**4. Reward-acquisition convenience & HUD accessibility**

◆ Advance warning for low Hongmoon Blessing — building a warning system, shown on
dungeon entry and before boss fights, to prevent the frustration of clearing a
hard dungeon only to lack the Blessing needed to open its reward box.

◆ Added a News tab to the HUD — for checking key in-game news/alerts without
leaving play.

**5. Last Stand Blackram Supply Chain event support**

◆ Extra event-coin distribution — many Daehyeop reported the coin requirement for
full clear rewards was too high for a repeat-heavy event, so beyond dungeon
clears we're adding a daily-login coin bonus (see [2026-08-28-neonotice.md](2026-08-28-neonotice.md)).
