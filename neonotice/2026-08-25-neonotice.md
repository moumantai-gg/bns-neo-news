# August 25, 2026 Boost Server FAQ

Greetings, Warriors.

## Boost Server Guide

**Q. What transfers and what doesn't when moving to the live server?**
A. On server transfer, everything except your currently equipped gear and any items
needed for quest progress is left behind.

*Transfers:*
- Currently equipped **Skill Books, Weapon, Accessories, Soul Badge, Mystic Badge,
  Soul Shield, Illusion Soul Shield, Costume, and Fated Encounter Stone**.
  - *Equipped gear and Skill Books become Bound on transfer; Bound equipped gear
    cannot be re-sealed.*
- Items required for quest progress
- Costumes held in your Wardrobe
- Achievements & titles earned
- Expanded storage/inventory space
- Quests currently held (in progress)
- Exploration Journal progress *(summed per region, using whichever of your
  account's characters on the same server has the highest progress in that region)*

*Does not transfer:*
- Every item other than the ones listed above, e.g.:
  - Unequipped Skill Books, gear
  - Costumes, boxes, and material items sitting in inventory
  - Gold held
  - Portraits held & completed Bonds/Collections
  - Equipped/held Seals
  - Mileage (Portrait Synthesis, Seal Combination, Transmute, Gear Enhancement)
  - Guild membership & guild-crafted items
  - Items currently in progress in a crafting commission
  - Unclaimed mail, etc.

**Q. How many times can Boost Mission daily/weekly missions be repeated?**
A. Up to 3 times for dailies, up to 1 time for weeklies, across the whole Boost
Server period.
- **Daily missions:** completable once per day, reset 5:00 AM daily, max 3
  completions total during the Boost Server period.
- **Weekly missions:** completable once per week, max 1 completion total during the
  Boost Server period.

**Q. Does the Boost Server give higher dungeon EXP or reward drop rates than the
Jin/Lyn servers?**
A. Dungeon EXP and rewards are identical between the Boost Server and the Jin/Lyn
servers.

**Q. Until when are event-collab Portrait Bonds/Collections valid?**
A. Usable until the October 28 regular maintenance. After that, any Bonds/Collections
that used a collab Portrait are removed.

**Q. If I complete an achievement on the Boost Server but don't claim its reward
before transferring, can I claim it on the Jin/Lyn server instead?**
A. No. An unclaimed achievement reward on the Boost Server is treated as already
claimed once you transfer, even though you never actually collected it — so it can't
be claimed on the Jin/Lyn server either. Claim your rewards on the Boost Server
before transferring.

**Q. After transferring to Jin/Lyn, can I succession a +10 Bound White Light item
into a tradable Gold Line item?**
A. No. If you held a +10 Bound White Light item on the Boost Server, it becomes a
"+10 Boost-Bound White Light item" after the Jin/Lyn transfer, and successioning
that item produces a **Bound Gold Line item**. Bound Gold Line items cannot be
traded.

| Boost Server | After Jin/Lyn transfer | After post-transfer succession |
|---|---|---|
| +10 White Light item | +10 Boost-Bound White Light item | Bound Gold Line item |

*Gold Line items themselves also become Bound Gold Line items when transferred to
the Jin/Lyn server.*

**Q. When does server transfer become available?**
A. The first transfer round runs after the August 26 regular maintenance, with
further rounds weekly through September 30 (6 rounds total).

**Q. How do I apply for server transfer?**
A. Once you reach 100% overall Boost Mission completion, click the "Apply for
Server Transfer" button in the upper right. You may apply up to 2 characters per
account; the transfer runs on whichever regular maintenance falls closest after
your application date.

*(A "Check Server Transfer Requirements" link is provided on the page itself.)*

**Q. The UI is bunching up in the top-left corner.**
A. Press SHIFT + F1 to open the UI edit window, then click Reset to fix it.

## Additional Guide

**Q. What should I do after logging into the Boost Server?**
A. Follow the yellow Epic quests. Once you reach Act 1, Chapter 4, a Path of
Growth icon appears on the right side of the screen — follow Path of Growth to the
end. *(Finishing Path of Growth completes every Epic quest up through the
Silverfrost Mountains.)*

**Q. What should I do after finishing Path of Growth?**
A. Click the Boost Mission icon on the right, find missions you can clear, and
keep growing by completing them.

**Q. What are the requirements to apply for Boost Server transfer?**
A. Complete 100% of all Boost Missions (repeatable missions excluded).
*(Reaching 100% requires +8 on both White Light weapon & accessory, and +8 on both
Sacred Soul Badge & Mystic Badge.)*

**Q. Where can I get Ductile Stone?**
A. Via NPC exchange, dungeon clears, or gear salvage:
- **NPC exchange:** F7 > Mushin's Tower > exchange a "Mushin's Tower Training
  Token" with the "Nameless Agent" NPC.
- **Dungeon clear:** F7 > Mushin's Tower > chance drop from clearing Mushin's
  Tower Floor 7, Silverfrost Trials, Trial Chamber, Training Chamber, or
  Reflection Chamber.
- **Gear salvage:** obtainable from salvaging non-Bound White-Light-or-above
  weapons/accessories.

**Q. Can I add a Soul Core socket to a weapon I already own, or a Mantra Stone
socket to an accessory I already own?**
A. Weapon Soul Core sockets can't be added to gear you already have; accessory
Mantra Stone sockets can be added using extra materials.
- **Weapon Soul Core:** cannot be added to an owned weapon — you need to farm a
  new weapon that already has a Soul Core socket.
- **Accessory Mantra Stone:** a socket can be added, with a chance of success,
  using a Superior Mantra Stone Hammer plus gold. *(The hammer is obtained by
  clearing Boost Missions, or with a chance from the Dawncrest Essence roulette
  wheel at Bright Night Garden.)*

**Q. Where do I use Hongmoon Essence?**
A. Left-click a weapon, accessory, or Soul Shield > Gear Management > Gear Effect
tab, and use it there to reset the item's effect — letting you reroll for the
substat you want.

**Q. When resetting a weapon/accessory/Soul Badge/Mystic Badge's Gear Effect or
Enhancement Effect, does the gold fee go up as the item's tier rises?**
A. Cost depends on both the tier and the item type.
- **Enhancement Effect reset cost:**
  - *(Gear)* Winterbane and White Light tiers cost the same; cost rises at the
    Gold Line tier.
  - *(Soul/Mystic Badge)* Cost rises going from Sacred to Dawncrest tier.
- **Gear Effect reset cost:**
  - *(Gear)* Cost rises at each tier step: Winterbane → White Light → Gold Line.
  - *(Soul Shield)* Cost rises going from Windshock to Sagewood tier.

**Q. Can Soul Shields be grown/leveled up?**
A. Yes — once you have a Soul Shield with the 2 substats you want, use either of
these two methods in the Soul Shield's Gear Management > Refine tab:
- **Growing via a same-slot Soul Shield:** sacrifice another Soul Shield from the
  same equipment slot; only the substat(s) shared between the sacrifice and your
  equipped Soul Shield increase in value.
  - *Example:* your equipped Windshock Soul Shield has HP / Crit / Crit Dmg as its
    2nd substat set; your sacrifice Soul Shield has HP / Accuracy / Evasion as its
    2nd substat set. Using the sacrifice only raises the HP substat on your
    equipped Soul Shield.
- **Soul Shield Primer fusion (Engraving):** grants stats separately from Soul
  Shield growth. Choose the Primer you want and fuse it in; fusing a different
  Primer onto a Soul Shield that already has one overwrites the existing Primer's
  stats.

**Q. Where do field bosses spawn?**
A. In one of three Dawncrest Isle areas: Forest of Wisdom, Earthbreaker Mountains,
or Divine Fist Wasteland.
- **Spawn notice:** a "A mysterious energy appears at ⟨region⟩" message shows
  before spawn. The field boss appears some time after the message, simultaneously
  on all 3 channels.
- **Getting there:** search the named region on the map (M) and teleport in. The
  field boss appears on either the east or west side of that region *(e.g. Divine
  Fist Wasteland: Ch.1 East, Ch.2 West, Ch.3 East)*. Its exact spot is marked on the
  minimap by the boss-monster icon.
