## 2. Ruler of the Land Takmaryeong Event

To celebrate the Ruler of the Land collaboration update, the GMs are coming to
visit you, Warriors! Team up with the GMs to defeat an even more powerful
"Takmaryeong" (탁마령) and earn special rewards!

**September 8 (Tue) – September 10 (Thu), 2026**

**[1] Defeat the Takmaryeong that appears in Nokmyeong Village during the event
period.**
Don't miss the "A mysterious energy appears" spawn line!
Location: Jeryonglim > Nokmyeong Village

**[2] When Takmaryeong is defeated, you can receive a special reward GM Hari has
prepared for you, Warriors.**

*All characters who participated in defeating "Takmaryeong"*
When the NPC boss "Takmaryeong" is defeated, every character who landed at least
one attack can receive a **Ruler of the Land Takmaryeong Box**, containing:
- GM Hari's Umakase Box (우마카세 상자)
- GM Hari's Byeolhokase Box (별호카세 상자)
- Lava Ox Head Challenge Box
- Lava Ox Body Challenge Box

*The character who lands the final hit on "Takmaryeong"*
The character who lands the final hit on the NPC boss "Takmaryeong" can receive an
**I Caught It! Box** (내가 잡았소 상자), containing:
- Lava Ox Head Challenge Box
- Lava Ox Body Challenge Box
- GM Hari's Umakase Box — choose 1 of 6 Cow Costume Boxes

**GM Hari's Byeolhokase Box** — choose 1 of 5 special titles:
- Guardian of Liberation (광복의 수호자)
- Clover Ding-Ding-Ding (클로버 딩딩딩)
- SEXY FOOD
- CHILL GUY
- Hunminjeongeum (훈민정음)

**Lava Ox Head/Body Challenge Box** — opening grants one of the listed items at a
set probability: Lava Ox Head/Costume, or Delivery Certificate: 1 Gold.

**[3] After defeating Takmaryeong, spend some time chatting with the GMs.**
Feel free to leave any questions you've had while playing, or anything you've
wanted to tell us about the game, through chat! From things you were curious
about to feedback you wanted to share, spend an enjoyable time chatting with the
GMs. We'll listen closely to Warriors' many voices and keep working to build an
even more fun and stable service.
