# August 27, 2026 Known Issues

Translated from the official [known issues notice](https://bns.plaync.com/board/neoupdate/view?articleId=6a8525cc6b722c561dc6a27b),
posted 2026-08-27 08:15 KST.

Greetings, Warriors.

We're informing you of currently known issues in Blade & Soul NEO.

**System > Mileage**
- Since the August 19 update, some rewards from the Legendary Portrait Synthesis
  mileage track have been missing.
  - *Fix planned for the September 2 (Wed) regular maintenance: the reward changes
    to a choice between the Legendary Portrait Selection Box and the Legendary
    Special Portrait Box.*
  - Daehyeop who already claimed the Legendary Portrait Synthesis mileage reward
    before that maintenance will have their previously granted "Special Portrait"
    reclaimed and reissued as the Legendary Portrait Selection Box.
  - Please file a 1:1 inquiry while still holding the claimed Special Portrait.
  - Items already used for collecting, synthesis, or Bond registration cannot be
    reissued.

We apologize for the inconvenience. Thank you.
