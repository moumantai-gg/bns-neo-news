## 1. Challenge Box Acquisition Condition Eased

So that character growth doesn't limit party play, we've eased the Challenge
Box acquisition conditions. Now characters with high Supreme Force and
characters still growing can freely form a party together.

**[1] Challenge Box Acquisition Condition Eased**
- Every party member's personal highest Supreme Force must meet or exceed the
  dungeon's minimum Supreme Force requirement, and
- If at least one party member's personal highest Supreme Force falls within
  the recommended Supreme Force range, the party can obtain the Challenge Box.

**[2] Frequency Adjusted with Reward Balance in Mind**
Keeping acquisition unlimited as before, with these eased conditions, could
affect the Challenge Box's reward value and overall reward balance — so we've
adjusted it so each character can obtain a Challenge Box up to 3 times per
day, regardless of dungeon type.
*(Obtaining a Challenge Box requires holding Hongmoon Blessing.)*
