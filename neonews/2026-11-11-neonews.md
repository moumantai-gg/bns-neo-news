## Update

### 1. Putting a Heart into the Ashen Wind

Special support for taking on Ashewind Cave, which so many people visit! Charge Dragonblood Hearts at the Dragon Coil set up in the dungeon, and take on Ashewind Cave.

**2026-11-11 (Wed) after regular maintenance ~ 11-18 (Wed) before regular maintenance**

*"We've prepared this so that more people can succeed in clearing Ashewind Cave!"*

**Hongjae's Gratitude**

Can be used to charge Dragonblood Hearts in the Ashewind Cave dungeon. It can be obtained in the Garden of Reflection and Moonrise Tourney Hall.

*Prompt: "Do you want to charge 1 Dragonblood Heart?" with the required item shown, and Confirm / Cancel buttons.*

**With a Dragonblood Heart charged, if your HP is fully depleted, 1 Heart is consumed and your HP is restored to 100%.**

*Message: "You spent a Dragonblood Heart to recover your HP."*

**Take on the Ghost Yomio fight together with your party using the restored HP!**

### 2. Moonrise Tourney Hall Difficulty Improvement

We've lowered the difficulty of Moonrise Tourney Hall's final boss, "M-ao", so it can be cleared more smoothly.

- Removed the Attack Power increase buff "Shadow Fury" that was granted to the boss NPC M-ao when the Dark Zone exploded, and lowered the damage of the Darkness Explosion.
- Changed it so that damage is applied each time Darkness Vulnerability is created on M-ao.

*Screen text: "The dark zone exploded."*

### 3. Fusion Mileage Reward Collection Convenience Improvement

Once Fusion Mileage rewards have accumulated, you can now check and receive the rewards directly in the Fuse window.

*Receive the reward without opening the separate Mileage popup, then go on to fuse!*

*Popup text: "Mileage rewards are available. Press [Receive] to continue combining." with a [Receive] button.*
