maintenance.

From Darkstorm Training Ground balance improvements to a wider Challenge
Reward range in Ashewind Cave! We've prepared a variety of improvements for
you, Warriors.

And don't forget the live broadcast at 8 PM on Thursday the 15th — join us!

## 1. Darkstorm Training Ground Balance Improved

We've lowered the attack power of Darkstorm Training Ground NPCs, and greatly
eased the HP recovery reduction effect, mainly for melee classes, with a
smaller easing for ranged classes as well.

We've improved this so that every class can fight through the dungeon more
smoothly.

## 2. Ashewind Cave Challenge Reward Range Expanded

We've adjusted the Challenge Reward conditions for Ashewind Cave, the 6-player
dungeon, so that more players can earn the Challenge Reward.

**Challenge Reward Condition Adjustment**

Condition: at least 1 party member at or below the listed value.

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Before | 40,700 or below |
| After | 43,900 or below |

## 3. Dragon Pillar Field Boss Location Display Improved

Each field boss's spawn location is now consolidated in one place, and the
Dragon Pillar (F7) has gained location-check and move functions, so it's even
easier to find and travel to field bosses.

## 4. Character Change Made More Convenient

We've improved convenience so you can switch characters directly, without going
through the waiting room.

*(Screenshot callout: System menu → Character Select.)*
