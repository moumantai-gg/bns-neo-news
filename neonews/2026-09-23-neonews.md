# September 23, 2026 Update News

Translated from the official [September 23 update news](https://bns.plaync.com/board/neonews/view?articleId=6ab25e89d430490e63f60f23)

Hello, this is NEO. 😊
We're here with the details of the update following the September 23 regular
maintenance. From the newly unveiled Dawncrest Isle Heroic dungeon "Ebondrake
Execution Grounds" to the various quality-of-life improvements previewed in the
Director's Letter — here's everything in today's update.

## 1. Dawncrest Isle Heroic Dungeon: Ebondrake Execution Grounds

**Dungeon Introduction**

> The Ebondrake Execution Grounds, stronghold of the Ritualists who execute
> the sect's dissenters. The Ebondrake remade an abandoned mine — one its
> undying fires never left — into a secret mine of their own, and intruders who
> set foot in the pyre at its deepest point will not escape punishment at the
> hands of the executioner Kumakage.

| Location | Party Size | Recommended Level |
|---|---|---|
| Dawncrest Isle > Bright Night Garden | 4 | 80 |

**Boss Introduction — Kumakage**

> Kumakage, the inquisitor who guards the depths of the Ebondrake Hideout.
> He unleashes powerful attacks using a massive weapon and flame, pressing the
> fight with blazes that blanket the battlefield and attacks that threaten to
> end it.

**Main Spoils** — clear the Ebondrake Execution Grounds to earn a range of
rewards that help you grow.

- Legendary Skill Book Chest
- Bloodshade Necklace / Bloodshade Bracelet
- Sagewood Soul Shield
- Mantra Stone: Jum
- Ritualist Executioner Kumakage
- Kumakage's Psyche
- Gold Line Necklace / Gold Line Bracelet
- Gold Line Steel / Gold Line Jade

Other spoils can be obtained as well.

## 2. Increased Winterbane Steel / Winterbane Jade Drop Rates

Winterbane Steel and Winterbane Jade drop rates are increased in Silverfrost
Mountains Superior and Heroic dungeons.

- **Winterbane Steel** — a Winterbane weapon enhancement material
- **Winterbane Jade** — a Winterbane accessory enhancement material

| Category | Target Dungeons |
|---|---|
| Silverfrost Mountains Superior Dungeons | Chuanka Frost Cavern |
| | Ebondrake Hideout |
| | Talus Dungeon |
| Silverfrost Mountains Heroic Dungeons | Sogun's Lament: Gardens |
| | Sundered Nexus: Upper Floor |

## 3. Supreme Force Delta Shown on Equipment Effect Change

Improved so that the Supreme Force increase or decrease is visible immediately
when changing an equipment effect.

## 4. Equipment Growth Quality-of-Life

Improved so that Essence Transfer and equipment effect resets can be performed
while the equipment is still worn.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Unequip the item, then transfer / reset effects | Transfer / reset effects with the item still equipped |

## 5. Dungeon Retry While Keeping the Party

Improved so that after clearing a dungeon you can restart it immediately with
the party intact — select "Retry" and re-enter the dungeon with your party
members right away.
