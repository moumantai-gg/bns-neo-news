### 2. Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery Potion cooldown removed

The reuse cooldown of the Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery Potion has been removed, so you can now use them back to back without waiting.

Applies to: Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery Potion, Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery Potion (Period), Lesser Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery Potion.

*Tooltip: "Hongmoon's Blessing Recovery: Using this restores 30 Hongmoon's Blessing. Instant."*

### 3. Improved convenience for checking Challenge rewards

We've made it easier to check whether Challenge rewards can be received. Check ahead of time whether the dungeon you're about to take on with your party still has Challenge rewards available!

The dungeon info window now shows one of these messages under the Challenge reward conditions:

- "There is a party member who meets the Challenge reward conditions."
- "There is no party member who meets the Challenge reward conditions."

*The images show the Red Sand Ruins (Legendary, 4 players) and the Ashewind Cave (Legendary, 6 players) as examples.*

### 4. Improved Illusion Soul Shield display option

An Illusion Soul Shield display ON/OFF button has been added, so you can freely choose whether it is shown.

*Click the button to toggle the Illusion Soul Shield display ON/OFF.*

### 5. Improved convenience for switching on the character select screen

On the character select screen, you can now double-click the character you want to log in with to enter right away, without pressing a separate select button.
