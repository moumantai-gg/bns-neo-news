### 7. Assorted Convenience Improvements

**① Spectator Mode added on death in dungeons**

If you die while clearing a dungeon, you can watch the fight from another party member's point of view. Watch your party members' play and learn boss patterns and how to counter them.

*Screen text: "You died, so you can spectate another character. Press Space to switch to the next party member's view."*

**② Boss-zone Dragon Vein activation improved**

When a party member enters the boss area, the Dragon Vein at the entrance now activates automatically. Move to the boss area conveniently through the Dragon Vein, without a separate escape process.

**③ Gold gained per dungeon redistributed**

Gold rewards are redistributed according to dungeon difficulty, so harder dungeons now give more gold.

| Dungeon | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Frenzy Avalanche Den | 2 Gold 40 Silver | 1 Gold 50 Silver (−90 Silver) |
| Phantasmic Beast Arena | 3 Gold 30 Silver | 2 Gold 30 Silver (−1 Gold) |
| Frozen Abandoned Mine | 4 Gold 50 Silver | 4 Gold (−50 Silver) |
| Garden of Reflection | 3 Gold 50 Silver | 4 Gold (+50 Silver) |
| Moonrise Tourney Hall | 3 Gold 50 Silver | 4 Gold 50 Silver (+1 Gold) |

*Based on the gold earned per dungeon clear.*

**④ Shop usage convenience improved**

When Soul Pass Plus benefits are active, you can now use materials stored in your warehouse directly in the shop, without moving them to your inventory.

**⑤ Item bidding convenience improved**

An amount-add button has been introduced so you can quickly adjust the party auction bid amount, making it easier to set the bid you want.
