# September 9, 2026 Update News

Translated from the official [September 9 update news](https://bns.plaync.com/board/neonews/view?articleId=6aa01031d430490e63f60e1b)
post, posted 2026-09-09 KST. Per this app's usual `neonews`-vs-`neoupdate`
distinction (see [2026-09-02-event-notice.md](2026-09-02-event-notice.md)),
this reads as a periodic **event/shop roundup** rather than official patch
notes, so it's filed under the **Event / Preview** category despite "Update"
in its title.

The source graphic listed 8 topic cards on its index page; all 8 have
accompanying detail panels below.

## 1. Dungeon Balance Improvements

Challenge reward conditions have been eased — you can now challenge dungeons
with a lower Supreme Force requirement.

**Dungeon Supreme Force Requirement Improvements**

| Dungeon | Party avg. Supreme Force (existing → changed) | Personal highest Supreme Force (existing → changed) |
|---|---|---|
| Ashewind Cave | 49,000 or below → 43,900 or below | 45,000 or above → 39,900 or above |
| Red Sand Ruins | 53,400 or below → 50,800 or below | 49,400 or above → 46,800 or above |

## 2. Last Stand Blackram Supply Chain — Difficulty Improvements

We've adjusted the difficulty of the Last Stand Blackram Supply Chain dungeon so
it's easier to clear.

Lowered Barracks Officer Mak Sobo's and Poharan's HP and regeneration, so the
fight can be cleared more comfortably.

*Take on the Last Stand Blackram Supply Chain event dungeon with newfound ease
before the September 16 (Wed) regular maintenance!*
